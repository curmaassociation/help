About The University

Central University of Rajasthan (CURAJ) is a central university located in Ajmer, Rajasthan, India, established in 2009 by an Act of Parliament. CURAJ has 12 schools encompassing 36 academic departments offering various undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs.

The university is located on the Jaipur-Ajmer Expressway (NH-8), approximately 83 kilometers from Jaipur and 40 kilometers from Ajmer. The campus spans more than 209 hectares (518 acres) of land with state-of-the-art infrastructure.

CURAJ follows the semester system and has adopted the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) as per UGC guidelines. The university emphasizes interdisciplinary research and innovation.

How to Reach CURAJ

By Air

Nearest Airport: Jaipur International Airport (JAI) - 85 km

From Jaipur airport, take a taxi to Sindri Bandar (₹1500-2000) or take a bus to Jaipur Bus Stand and then a bus to Bandar Sindri (₹100). From Bandar Sindri, take an auto to university (₹50-100).

By Train (Option 1)

Direct Train: Marusagar Express from Ernakulam to Ajmer (Weekly)

From Ajmer Junction, take a share taxi to Bus Stand (₹20), then bus to Bandar Sindri (₹50). Autos available from Bandar Sindri to campus.

By Train (Option 2)

Connecting Trains: Via Kota, Ahmedabad or Marwar Junction

Take any train to these stations, then connect to Ajmer. Follow directions from Ajmer as above.

By Road

From Jaipur: Buses available from Sindhi Camp Bus Stand to Bandar Sindri (every 30 mins)

From Ajmer: Regular buses to Bandar Sindri (30-40 mins journey)

Admission Process

Admission Process

  1. Check eligibility criteria for desired course
  2. Fill out the application form before deadline
  3. Pay application fee
  4. Appear for entrance exam (if applicable)
  5. Check merit list/cutoff
  6. Complete document verification
  7. Pay admission fees
  8. Complete registration process

Admission Requirements

Documents Needed

  • 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates
  • Graduation marksheets (for PG courses)
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate (if applicable)
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (for scholarship)
  • Passport size photographs- 3
  • Valid ID proof (Aadhar, Passport, etc.)
  • Medical Certificate ( govt. approved )
  • Category Certificate (NCL)-(if applicable)
  • Gap Certificate (if applicable)
  • CUET ScoreCard
